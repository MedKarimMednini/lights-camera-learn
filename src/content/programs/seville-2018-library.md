Seville 2018
...brought us into the heart of Spain! Our smallest program of 2018 (13 students and 2 films) and our first in Spain, Seville 2018 was bright, colorful and inspirational. Since our arrival in the country, we were immersed in the heat and vibrance of the Spanish summer. We explored the wonders of flamenco and the deliciousness of tapas before buckling down to work with our students. In just five days, we wrote, produced, edited, and screened two original short films. Check out our work from the height of the summer!
