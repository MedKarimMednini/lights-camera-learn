Nazareth 2018...
...was our biggest session ever. We brought together over 110 kids from 5 different schools to participate in two different programs over the course of ten days. By the Red Carpet event, we had produced ten original short films across all genres, from adventure to comedy, documentary to vlog. Through challenges and triumphs, we learned a valuable piece of wisdom.
“Life is like a camera. Focus on what's important. Develop the negatives.
Capture the good times. And if things don't work out, just take another shot.”
